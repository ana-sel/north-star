# Compass — "You" universe · interaction & content spec

> **Guidelines, not rules.** This document records the current best thinking about
> what each screen does. It is not the source of truth — the design is. When a
> wireframe and this spec disagree, fix the design first, then update this file to
> match. Treat everything here as a strong suggestion that must still earn its place.
> Covers the four You tabs: **Paths · Identity · Discover · Progress**.
> Companion specs (Today, Log, Plan, Money) follow the same format.
> **Read [`spec-00-principles.md`](spec-00-principles.md) alongside this.**

## 0. The coaching model (why this exists)

Compass helps you **become a grounded, authentic adult** and grow the qualities you're drawn to — by recognising them as **seeds already in you**, not as things you lack in others. (See principles §2.5 — admiration is a mirror; §1.4 — no rumination/comparison.) The app's job is to **name → define → show where you are → offer the practice → let *you* confirm integration.**

Every growable thing (Quality, Emotional tool, Path) moves through the same **integration states**:

| State | Meaning | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **Drawn to** | A seed you've noticed in yourself. Capped, non-hoardable | tiny "drawn to" shelf (≤ 3) |
| **Practising** | Active — a daily cue **or** a Path behind it (either is fine) | Identity + Paths + Today |
| **Integrated** | **You** confirmed it's part of you now — permanent | Identity (gold), Progress |
| **Paused** | Temporarily set down, history kept, no pressure | Identity (dimmed) |

**"Drawn to" is anti-limerence by design.** It is *not* a wishlist of what you lack. When you notice a quality you're drawn to, Compass reframes it (*“this is a seed already in you”*) and offers only two doors: **practise it now** or **let it rest**. Hard cap of 3 so it can never become a rumination shelf.

**Coaching = curated choice.** Libraries are opinionated and *capped* (see limits). You can always **+ Add custom**, but the app keeps focus narrow — you can only deliberately grow a few things at once, and that's healthy.

### 0.1 Earning is self-claimed, never app-awarded (see §4 of principles)
A practice/Path completing does **not** auto-earn a quality. Compass asks: **“Does this feel like you now?”**
- **Yes, part of me** → becomes Integrated (permanent).
- **Not yet — still growing** → *no loss, no penalty.* “Good — honesty is the practice.” Keeps quietly going and **eases** tracking pressure; optional one-liner: “what will it look like when it's true?”
- **I'm not sure** → extends the observation window; leans to your pace.

### 0.2 A quality can grow with or without a Path
Forming a quality may sit behind a **Path** (a full journey with camps) **or** just a **gentle daily cue**. Not everything needs a journey.

### 0.3 The hierarchy & the one-home rule (the untangle)
Everything in the You universe fits **one** hierarchy — one concept, one home:

```
Tools  →  Journeys  →  Qualities  →  Identity
(atoms)   (the work)   (who you       (who you
                        become)         are)
```
- **Tools** = atomic skills/practices (breathing, NVC, self-compassion). They are *used by* journeys — never a journey themselves.
- **Journeys** = the only "paths." Two kinds: **Foundations** (the floor, tiered) and **Growth paths** (chosen, grouped by domain). Each grows one or more **Qualities**.
- **Qualities** = the character outcome a journey builds.
- **Identity** = where earned qualities + integrated tools + the durable self live.

### 0.4 A growth path has no name of its own
A growth path grows **exactly one quality**, so naming the path separately just says
the same word twice ("The Stillness Path grows Stillness"). **The quality is the item.**
A path is the *structure* — three camps, a ritual, a practice — not a second object.

Say it once. In the UI a path is shown as: **domain · state · quality · how it shows up.**

**The four domains** are defined by *where the quality is exercised*, so they don't overlap.
(The old set — Inner / Relational / Craft & mastery / Spirit / Material — failed: `Inner` and
`Spirit` were indistinguishable, and `Material` was empty once goals moved to Plan.)

| Domain | The question it answers | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Inward** | How you meet yourself | Stillness, Presence |
| **Together** | How you meet other people | Warmth, Quiet authority |
| **Craft** | How you meet the work | Craftsmanship |
| **Frontier** | How you meet what is new | Spirit of discovery |

**Colour belongs to the domain, not the quality** — so the palette teaches the taxonomy
instead of decorating individual cards.

**Vocabulary is capped at three meanings and two mechanics.** Value / Virtue / Quality /
Grows were four words for one idea and must not return.

| Word | Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Quality** | the trait being grown — stated once, never re-defined | Stillness |
| **Shows up as** | how you'd recognise it **in your life**. Never a synonym of the quality | "You pause before you react, instead of after." |
| **Sounds like** | the two inner sentences — see §0.5 | “I’m hopeless at this” → “Huh, interesting how that fits” |
| **Why it matters** | the value underneath it | Peace — being, not doing, has worth |
| **Ritual** | a daily habit. **Optional**, and **may be shared by several paths.** Owned by Log · Habits | 1 min silence on waking |
| **Practice** | the spaced deliberate act — a few times a week, never two days running | 5-min sit before your first screen |

> ❌ "Calm, unhurried, at home in silence" is a dictionary definition of *stillness*. It restates the headline and teaches nothing.
> ✅ "You pause before you react, instead of after." is observable, and tells you what changes.

**Material outcomes are not qualities.** Buying land is a **goal** and lives in
**Plan · Goals**. What belongs in Paths is the quality that carries you there —
Patience, Stewardship.

### 0.4a The cap is on commitment, not on the library
Two different limits get confused:

| | Limit |
|---|---|
| How many you **practise at once** | Hard cap of **3**. This is the anti-overwhelm rule (§1.2) |
| How many **exist to recognise yourself in** | **Comprehensive.** No cap |

A truncated library isn't discipline — you cannot choose well from a list that is missing
pages. Cap the commitment; keep the map complete.

### 0.4b Domain says where; "what it buys you" says why
A domain (Inward / Together / Craft / Frontier) tells you where a quality is exercised.
It does not tell you what it is *for*. So every quality also carries the life aims it serves:

| Aim | Means |
|---|---|
| **Freedom** | money, autonomy, options |
| **Peace** | steadiness, non-reactivity |
| **Love** | reciprocal adult relationships |
| **Aliveness** | curiosity, creativity, an interesting life |
| **Mastery** | skill, discipline, execution |

A quality serving **three or more** is a leverage point, and Browse offers a
**Highest leverage** filter for exactly that set. This is objective structure, not a score —
never combine the aims into one number, and never rank a person by them (§20 of the
master context: no single "how good am I" score).

**Financial freedom is not slow by definition.** Compounding applies to skill, products and
ownership, not only to index funds — building a thing once that keeps earning is the
*fastest* honest route, and Craft holds the qualities that make it possible (Leverage,
Shipping, Rare skill, Deciding under uncertainty, Calculated risk). What the model rules
out is **bad sizing**, not speed: `Calculated risk` exists precisely so a fast move can be
taken without it being a gamble.

### 0.4c Two taxonomies, and neither may repeat the other
Browse offers three filters, each answering a different question:

| Filter | Asks | How it's set |
|---|---|---|
| **Core to you** | *What matters most to me?* | **The user sets it.** Never assigned by the app, and never named after a reason |
| **Highest leverage** | *What pays into three lives at once?* | Computed from the aims |
| **Domain** | *Where is this exercised?* | Fixed |

> **A domain already implies one aim, so the card must not repeat it.** Every Inward quality
> serves Peace (9 of 9), every Together one serves Love (12 of 12), every Frontier one serves
> Aliveness (6 of 6). Showing that tag again is a label carrying zero information. Cards show
> only what a quality pays into **beyond its own home** — *"also pays into · Peace · Mastery"*.
> The detail sheet names the home aim and says why it is implied.

| Domain | Implied aim |
|---|---|
| Inward | Peace |
| Together | Love |
| Craft | Freedom |
| Frontier | Aliveness |

**Colour ceiling: four domains + gold.** Gold means *earned*, and only earned — the one place
colour signals **state** rather than **place**. Aims stay as text; five more colours would
collapse the system into decoration.

**Integrated qualities are read-only and leave Browse.** You cannot start something that has
already become part of you. They show as gold chips — *"Claimed by you, not awarded by
Compass"* — on Paths and in Identity.

### 0.4d Core is not the maturity checklist
**Foundations** is what every adult needs and nobody was taught. **Core** is a personal
shortlist the user sets, for reasons they never have to state. Do not conflate them, and do not
let Core drift into a "healthy adult" scorecard — that turns a shortlist you choose from into a
standard you fall short of.

### 0.5 The quality changes in the narration, not the visible act
Two people assemble the same IKEA wardrobe for the same hour with the same fumbling.
One is thinking *“I’m hopeless at this — who wrote these instructions?”*; the other
*“Huh, interesting how that part fits. I’ll find a way.”* The observable behaviour is
identical. **Only the second one is practising anything.**

So every quality carries a **voice pair**, shown inside the practice block:

| Field | Framing | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **When it's tight** | the automatic sentence | Describe a **state**, never a character flaw. Not "your wrong thought" |
| **The one you're practising** | the sentence being offered | Concrete, in the user's own idiom, short enough to actually recall mid-task |

- Both sentences live in the user. The practice is **noticing which one is speaking**, not eliminating one.
- Never mark the first with an ✗ or red — that is deficit framing (principles § prohibited).
- The voice pair is **not a separate section.** It belongs inside the practice, because saying the sentence *is* the practice.

**The sentence appears on Today, not only in Paths.** Paths is where you understand it; Today is
the moment you need it. The anchor hero carries the practising sentence under *“Say this to
yourself”*, and it swaps with the quality.

> **The rotating cue must not contain a quoted sentence.** The cue says *when and where*
> (“when the pressure rises, notice your shoulders before you notice the problem”); the voice
> block owns *the words*. Two competing sentences on one card read as noise — the original
> Today copy had *“When pressure rises, say to yourself: ‘I have time. I can choose.’”* directly
> above a different sentence to say to yourself.

### 0.5a A practice must be executable, and must not be failable
"5-min sit before your first screen" names a **container**, not the work. It leaves the
user asking *“and then what — just sit?”* Every practice therefore has three parts:

| Part | Answers | Example (Stillness) |
|---|---|---|
| **What + how often** | the container | "Sit for 5 minutes, before your first screen" · 2–4× / week |
| **Do this** | the actual attentional move, **including saying the sentence** | "… you will drift into planning. Each time you catch it, say the sentence and come back. **The catching-and-returning is the practice. Not the sitting.**" |
| **It counts when** | an outcome the user **controls** | "You came back **once**. Not when your mind goes quiet — it won't, and that was never the goal." |

**"It counts when" is the safety mechanism.** It must name something the user can do and
explicitly rule out the outcome they can't:

| Quality | Counts when | Explicitly *not* |
|---|---|---|
| Craftsmanship | you did the last tenth once | when the result is good |
| Quiet authority | you stopped after saying it once | when they agree |
| Warmth | you said it out loud | when they respond well |
| Spirit of discovery | you started before you felt ready | when it goes well |

Without this line, every practice has a hidden failure condition the user will invent for
themselves — and it will be the outcome, which is never theirs.

### 0.7 The three camps are named once, not thirteen times
Every path has the same shape, so the camps carry the **same three names everywhere**:

| Camp | Name | What is true in it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Watching** | You only notice. **No practice yet** — seeing the pattern is the whole work |
| 2 | **Doing** | The practice starts |
| 3 | **Under pressure** | Does it hold when it's actually hard? |

The earlier design gave each path its own camp names — *First Contact, Deepening, Embodied,
Attention, Consistency, Mastery, Groundwork, Venturing, Wonder…* Thirteen invented proper
nouns to learn, inconsistent in kind (a stage, a quality and an outcome side by side), and
carrying nothing that "Camp 2 of 3" didn't already say.

Shared names also act as a **consistency check on the content**: they immediately exposed a
path sitting in "Watching" whose camp-1 copy already prescribed a practice, and two camp-3
"tests" that tested nothing under pressure.

**Camp 3 must be a real test under pressure** — tired, watched, in conflict, when it costs
something. "Let yourself be genuinely amazed" is not a test.

### 0.8 Constant information is no information
A label identical on every card teaches nothing and costs a line. The card previously carried
a `PRACTISING` state badge on every active path — but Integrated qualities never appear as
cards, so it never varied. Cut. The same rule retired "Integrated · 5 wks" chips: the number
was ambiguous (time taken? time held?) and an integrated quality is part of you, not a score.

### 0.8a A number typed twice will eventually disagree with itself
Every count shown to the user derives from one structured field. "27 returns · 4 weeks ·
1 camp closed" was hand-typed on the card *and* re-typed inside the claim moment — and had
already drifted (the returns trail's last mark said *today* while the card said *yesterday*).

Camp-completion and quality-claim moments render from the path's data, never from fixed copy:
the stage name, the counts, the quality name and the toast all come from the record.

**No emoji.** The design system is line icons throughout (`shared/you.css`); the moments used
🌿 and ✨, which is the only place emoji had crept in. Gold stays reserved strictly for *earned*.

### 0.9 "How many times until it's mine?" — answer honestly, refuse to set a target
There is no threshold, and inventing one would be both false and coercive. Lally et al.
(2010) found automaticity reached anywhere between **18 and 254 repetitions**, averaging
~66 — *the spread is the finding.*

**And a quality is not a habit.** A habit is cue-triggered; a disposition shows up across
*varied* situations. The repetition research does not transfer, so the claim moment asks about
**breadth of context** and **unplanned occurrence** — the markers actually supported — rather
than a count. Self-determination theory (Deci & Ryan) is the reason this matters: an
externally-imposed target undermines the intrinsic motivation it is meant to support.

The claim moment presents four statements to **read, not tick**. Nothing counts them:

1. It has shown up when you were tired or stretched.
2. It has cost you something, and you did it anyway.
3. It has happened when nobody would ever have known.
4. **It has happened without you planning it.**

> The fourth is the marker. *Doing it on purpose is practice. Finding that you have already
> done it is the quality.*

The repetition count still appears, demoted to a footnote: *"27 returns · 4 weeks · 3 camps —
context, not count."*

- ❌ a progress bar toward 66, a "42 to go" counter, tickboxes that total, or any completion percentage.
- ✅ a plain count of returns beside the honest range, with the claim left entirely to the user (§0.1).
- Camp-level counters ("4 more practices open Camp 3") are fine — a camp is a real, near, deadline-free step. **Integration is never counted.**

**The four tabs = four verbs:**

| Tab | Verb | Question | Owns |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paths** | Doing | What am I walking? | Journeys (Foundations + Growth) — only interactive journey home |
| **Identity** | Being | Who am I? | Values · Qualities (mirror) · integrated Tools · skills · education · people |
| **Discover** | Reflecting | What am I learning? | Check-ins · AI patterns · deep question · stable self-knowledge |
| **Progress** | Measuring | How's it going? | Charts · streaks · goals — data only |

**One-home ownership (lives in / appears read-only in):**

| Concept | Lives in | Appears (read-only) in |
|---|---|---|
| Journeys · Foundations · Growth paths | **Paths** | Progress (momentum), Today (anchor) |
| Qualities — forming | **Identity** (mirror of Paths) | Paths (what a path builds) |
| Qualities — earned | **Identity** (permanent) | Progress |
| Values · skills · education · people | **Identity** | — |
| Tools — full library | reference sheet | opened from Identity / Paths / Foundations |
| Tools — integrating / integrated | **Identity** (capabilities) | Today (if it has a daily cue) |
| Reflection · patterns · check-ins | **Discover** | — |
| Numbers · charts · streaks · goals | **Progress** | — |

**De-dup rule:** a concept that reads like several things lives **once**. Boundaries = a *Tool* (taught inside the Communicate foundation); Grief = the *Handle-hard-things* foundation journey; Stillness = a *Quality* grown by an Inner growth path. Each is surfaced elsewhere read-only, never duplicated.

## 1. Shared interaction conventions

These apply across all four tabs so behaviour is consistent.

### 1.1 The "Show all / Add more" picker
Any `+ Add` or `Show all` opens a **library sheet** (bottom sheet on phone):
- Items **grouped by family**, with a **search** field at top.
- Each row: name + one-line definition; tap the row → expands the full definition, "shows up as", and practices.
- Primary action per row: **Add** (→ your set). If already added: **Added ✓** (tap to remove).
- Sheet header shows your **current count vs. limit** (e.g. "Forming 2 / 3").
- Closing: swipe down, tap outside, or **Done**.

### 1.2 The active-focus budget (the anti-overwhelm rule)
The real constraint isn't how many things you've *chosen* — it's **how many daily practices land on Today.** Foundations, Growth paths, quality cues and tools all create daily cues, so they **share one budget**, never separate stacking caps.

**Active focus = 3 daily touchpoints max — ideally 1 primary + 2 light.**
- **Primary** (1): the journey you're really walking — a Foundation *or* a Growth path — with its anchor habit.
- **Light** (≤ 2): small supporting cues — a quality practice or a foundation habit.
- "Active" = shows a cue on **Today**. Everything else (Drawn-to / Saved / Paused / Not-started) shows **nothing** on Today.
- Adding a 4th doesn't block — Compass asks with care: *“Your hands are full. Want to let something settle or finish first?”* → **Pause** · **Complete** · or swap.
- Pacing (Middle Way, principles §11): favour **one primary at a time**, deepened to a *steadier* self-claimed point before adding another. Adding sooner is allowed but meets a gentle reflective pause, never a wall.

**Other (non-daily) caps stay — these don't touch Today:**
| Set | Limit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Core **Values** | 7 | Values are few by definition |
| **Drawn to** (seeds) | 3 | Not a wishlist — anti-rumination/limerence |
| **Energy: gives / drains** | 8 each | Sharp self-knowledge, not a dump |
| **Skills / People / Education** | soft (≤ 12) | Reference lists, not focus lists |

> Supersedes the old per-type caps (Active Paths 3 / Qualities forming 3 / Tools 3) — those **stacked to 9 daily cues**, which is exactly the overwhelm we're avoiding.

### 1.3 Delete / remove behaviour (universal rules)
| Item type | On delete |
|---|---|
| **No history** (just added, never practised) | Remove immediately, no confirm |
| **Has history** (practising, has progress) | Confirm sheet → **Pause** (keep history, dim) · **Archive** (hide, keep data) · **Delete** (destroys history — red, requires hold-to-confirm) |
| **Integrated / Earned** | **Cannot be deleted** (it's part of you). Can **Hide** from the active portrait only |
| **Active Path** | "Abandon path?" → warns streak + camp progress is lost; offers **Pause** instead |
| **Completed Path** | Cannot delete — it has become an Earned quality |

### 1.4 Lens header (every You screen)
Read-only orientation: icon badge + tab name + purpose line. **Not** interactive.

### 1.4a Card-shaped controls must be reachable without a mouseA `div` with `onclick` is invisible to keyboard and screen-reader users. Any tappable card
(active path, browse card, suggestion) needs `role="button"`, `tabindex="0"` **and** an
Enter/Space handler — `role` without the handler is worse than nothing, because it announces
a button that cannot be pressed. Every sheet closes on **Escape**.

Text on the dark path cards sits at or above **0.62 opacity**; 9–10px type at 0.4–0.5 on a dark
gradient is not readable, however elegant it looks in a mock.

### 1.5 Energy contract (one stream, different lenses)

Energy is one stream of timestamped observations, not a separate score owned by every screen.

| Concept | Lives in | Appears in | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Energy check-in** | **Log · Energy** | Sleep, Today, Progress | Usable energy now, 1–10, with timestamp and source |
| **Morning energy** | **Log · Sleep** | Today, Energy timeline, Progress | The first check-in of the day, linked to the sleep entry |
| **Energy shift** | **Log · Energy** | Discover, Progress | Before/after observation around an activity, person, or context |
| **Suggested pattern** | **Discover** | — | A repeated association Compass offers for review, never a causal claim |
| **Confirmed energy knowledge** | **Discover** | Energy map (read-only) | A pattern the user explicitly accepts as true for them |
| **Energy trend** | **Progress** | — | Factual history and coverage, without interpretation |
| **Pace response** | **Today** | — | Optional Lighter/Rest suggestion based on the latest state |

#### Check-ins and shifts

- A check-in records `value (1–10) · timestamp · source · optional context`.
- Saving sleep with morning energy creates or updates that sleep entry's linked morning check-in. It does **not** create a duplicate Energy record.
- A later check-in adds a new point to the day. It never overwrites the morning observation.
- Optional dimensions are **Body · Emotional · Mental clarity · Meaning**. They describe context; they are not four mandatory scores and are never averaged into an authoritative composite.
- A shift may record `before · after · activity/person/context · optional dimensions · judgment`.
- Shift judgments are **Restores me · Costs energy but matters · Costs too much · Neutral**. Meaningful effort must not be classified as a harmful drain merely because it costs energy.

#### Pattern lifecycle

```
Observed → Suggested pattern → Seems true → Confirmed self-knowledge
                           ↘ More evidence → keep observing
                           ↘ Not true for me → dismiss
```

- Suggested patterns show the observation count, typical shift, date range, contexts, and known exceptions.
- Correlation is described as association: “often followed by”, “may”, or “seems”. Compass does not claim that an activity caused a state.
- A pattern enters **Confirmed self-knowledge** only after explicit confirmation. Evidence alone never promotes it automatically.
- Confirmed `Restores me` and `Costs energy` sets remain capped at **8 each**. A custom item may be confirmed directly, but is labelled **Noted by you** rather than evidence-backed.
- `More evidence` keeps the candidate quiet until meaningfully new observations exist. `Not true for me` dismisses it without penalty.

#### Response and recovery

- Low energy may prompt Today to offer **Lighter** or **Rest**, with the current state as context. The user chooses; Compass never changes pace automatically.
- Dismissing the suggestion or keeping **Full** produces no warning, penalty, or repeated prompt for the unchanged state.
- Recovery suggestions are invitations matched to available time and the user's context. They do not diagnose a cause or prescribe treatment.
- Only one energy experiment may be active at a time. An experiment has a hypothesis, observation condition, duration, and explicit finish/stop action.
- Task-level H/M/L costs, calendar capacity warnings, and automatic energy budgeting are deferred beyond the first design pass.

## 2. PATHS  (`plan-trails.html`) — "What am I walking?"

The **only** interactive home for paths and camps. It does **not** own habits — see §2.3.

### 2.1 Element interaction spec
| Element | On click | Opens / goes to | Shows | Limit | On delete |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lens header | — | — | "Paths · journeys you're walking now" | — | — |
| Section "Active paths · N of M" | — | — | count of active vs allowed | max **3** active | — |
| **Active path card** (whole card) | Opens **Path detail sheet** | sheet slides up | see §2.2 | — | via detail → Abandon (§1.3) |
| **Earned** chips | Tap → quality detail | Identity → that quality | name + weeks | — | cannot delete (permanent) |
| Section "Available paths" | — | — | browsable library (curated) | — | — |
| **Available path card** | Opens **Path detail sheet** (preview mode) | sheet | what it works on, duration, camps, "Start path" | — | — |
| Locked path (🔒) | Tap → explains unlock condition | toast/sheet | "Unlocks after Boundaries" | — | — |
| **Show all paths** (add) | Opens **Paths library** picker | library sheet | all paths grouped by family (§5.3) | start respects max 3 | — |
| Detail sheet → **Start path** | Activates path | back to Active list; path appears; Today gets anchor | confirmation + first camp | if at 3 active → "Replace…" | — |
| Detail sheet → **See it on Today** | Jumps to Today | Today screen, practice highlighted | — | — | — |
| Camp **completion celebration** | Appears on camp finish | full-screen overlay | camp message + quote + "Continue to Camp N" | — | dismiss only |
| Path **complete** (final camp) | Path retires → becomes Earned quality | moves to Earned shelf + Identity | "X is now part of you" | — | n/a (permanent) |

### 2.2 What each surface shows

**Active path card — orientation only.** Seven elements, no more:

```
┌ You came back twice since you were last here. ──────┐   ← only when true
│ ☾ INWARD                                            │
│ Stillness                                           │
│ I am becoming someone who can rest without earning  │
│ it.                                                 │
│ ●━━━━━━━━━◉╌╌╌╌○╌╌╌╌◌                              │   ← the route
│ Camp 2 of 3 · Doing ›          Last practised yest. │
│ 4 more practices open "Under pressure"              │
│ ─────────────────────────────────────────────────── │
│ RITUAL  1 min silence on waking      in Log · Habits│
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Quality is the headline**, said once. No path name, no kicker, no repeat in the eyebrow.
- **The identity line, not "shows up as", is on the card.** *"I am becoming someone who…"* is the point of the whole feature; it was buried at 12px two taps deep. This creates a deliberate asymmetry — **Browse tells you what you'd observe; Walking tells you who you're becoming.**
- **The route replaces the progress bar** (§2.2a).
- **Ritual line is omitted entirely** when the path has no habit.
- **No checkbox.** Doing happens on Today; this screen orients.

### 2.2a The route — the expedition has to be visible
Camps, paths, walking and terrain were words attached to a 5px progress bar. The card now
draws the journey:

- **Four nodes**: three camps plus a **claim node** at the end, ringed in gold and dashed — the
  one you reach and then decide about yourself (§0.1). It is never filled by the app.
- **Walked portion** solid in the domain colour; the rest a faint bed.
- **A walker** at `(camps closed + reps ÷ repsTotal) ÷ campTotal`. It derives only from real
  returns, so it can never move backwards on a bad week.

Rejected: a tick per return along the line. The returns trail already lives in the sheet and
ticks made the card noisy — the walker's position is the honest signal.

### 2.2b The reward is news that is true
Removing the anchor checkbox was right (§2.3), but it left nothing satisfying behind. On an
orientation screen the only honest reward is **something that actually happened while you were
away**: a ribbon reading *"You came back twice since you were last here."*

- Rendered **only when the count is non-zero**. Never a placeholder, never "0 returns".
- Warm and factual. Not `+2`, not a badge, not a score.
- The walker being visibly further along than last time is the second half of the reward.

### 2.2c A card in "Watching" has less to say, and says so
Form does not vary by stage — that would be decoration. **Content does.** A path in Camp 1 has
no practice, so it shows no practice counter: *"No practice yet — this camp only asks you to
notice."* Honest differentiation, no extra styling.

### 2.2d Required states
| State | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Empty** (0 paths) | The first screen a new user meets. Explains the model in one sentence, offers one primary action, and points to Foundations. No shame, no empty grid |
| **1–2 paths** | Normal, with `+ Add path` |
| **At capacity** (3) | The add button becomes a note, not a block: *"Your hands are full. Let one settle or finish before adding another — three at a time is the point, not a limit to beat."* (§1.2) |
| **Resting** (≥ 7 days) | **Visually identical.** Only the copy softens to *"Resting — nothing's lost"* and the news ribbon disappears. Dimming a neglected card would be a punishment |
| **Finished** | Final camp done → the claim moment (§0.1) |

**One thing, one place, in every state.** A quality that is active must not also appear in
Browse or on the seed shelf. Both derive from the same `activeIds()`, never from a separate flag.

**Path detail sheet — all depth lives here.** One disclosure level; there is no inline card expand.
Five blocks, no more. Each earns its place; anything that restates another block is cut.
1. **Hero**: domain + its question, quality, the identity statement (*"I am becoming someone who…"*), value tags.
2. **What has shifted** (active paths only): returns + weeks + camps closed, one observed association, the **returns trail** (§2.4), and the honest repetition range (§0.9).
3. **The practice** (§0.5a): what + how often · do this · the voice pair · it counts when · the ritual line.
4. **What this quality is**: Shows up as · Inner work. *(Two rows. "Why it matters" is already the hero's value tags.)*
5. **How it unfolds**: 3 camps, each with what it produces.
6. **CTA**: `Start this path` (or `See it on Today`) + secondary `Save as a seed instead`.

> **Cut and must not return:** an effort stat-grid (duplicates the practice's own "what + how
> often"); a paragraph explaining ritual-vs-practice (the practice block shows it); a note
> telling the user that goals belong in Plan (it lectures about the app's taxonomy, and
> nobody asked while browsing).

**Browse — a field guide, not a shop.** Walking-now cards are **dark**: they are yours, personal,
in progress. Browse cards are **light**, with a domain-coloured spine — possibilities you might
recognise. Dark = yours, light = possible. The section is headed *"Which of these do you
recognise?"*, not "Qualities you could grow" — per the mirror principle, admiration is
recognition of a seed already in you, not a purchase.

Domains are filters, not headings. Life areas are worth showing, but section headers per domain
leave dead empty sections once a domain's qualities are all active. Instead:
- a scrollable row of **domain chips** (icon + name) filters one flowing list;
- **every domain always gets a chip.** An empty one explains itself — *"You're already practising Craftsmanship here"* — rather than disappearing, so a domain shown on a card always has a matching chip;
- each card carries its **own domain badge** (icon + name) plus a `3 camps` pill, so it stays self-describing when the filter is `All`;
- the **suggested** quality is excluded from the list below it. Nothing appears twice on one screen.

| Domain | Icon | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| Inward | `ic-moon` | `#9b8fd6` |
| Together | `ic-users` | `#cf8f94` |
| Craft | `ic-pen` | `#d3aa62` |
| Frontier | `ic-compass` | `#7fa8b8` |

Duration on a browse card reads **`3 camps`**, not a week count. Camps open on
**returns, not dates**, so any advertised week figure is a promise the app can't keep.
The rough span belongs in the camps section, framed as an estimate: *"Three camps, about
6 weeks at this rhythm. There's no clock — camps open on returns, not dates."*

### 2.3 Paths do not own habits
A **ritual** is a habit. Habits are owned by **Log · Habits**, and:
- a path **may have none** — plenty of qualities grow on spaced practice alone;
- **two paths may share one ritual** — one morning silence can serve Stillness and Presence.

Therefore the path card shows a ritual as a **read-only relationship**, never a checkbox. A
checkbox here would create a second source of truth and double-count a shared habit.

The card's ritual line reads `RITUAL · <habit>` with `in Log · Habits` on the right — which
tells you where to act. It does **not** say "shared": at that moment you don't need to know how
many paths use the habit, and the word reads as "shared with whom?".

**A path's practice must not also appear in the ritual list on Today.** The spaced practice is
the anchor hero; only the daily ritual belongs in the morning/evening rings. Today previously
listed "5-min silent sit" as a morning ritual node *and* as the theme practice above it — the
same act, twice on one screen, on two different rhythms.

**Today's quality card rotates only through qualities from paths you are actually walking.** It
previously listed Presence and Patience, neither of which was active, and cited a path that no
longer exists. Horizontal swiping moves only among eligible qualities and excludes the quality
practised yesterday; tapping the card opens that quality's read-only path details.

### 2.4 Progress display must not manufacture pressure
Practice is **deliberately spaced** — 2–4× a week, never two days running — and Today allows a
deliberate skip. So a calendar grid of "last 14 days" renders the design working as **13 visible
failures**. Empty cells read as accusations.

| ❌ Never | ✅ Instead |
|---|---|
| A 14-cell grid with empty slots | A **returns trail** — a hairline with a mark only where you returned. Nothing is drawn for a rest day |
| "5 of last 7 days" (a ratio you can fall short of) | "Last practised yesterday" |
| "12 days since you practised" | After a week, stop counting: **"Resting — nothing's lost"** |
| Red, warnings, or a broken-streak state | Nothing. A gap is rest |

Always pair the trail with the reason: *"Practice is spaced on purpose — never two days running —
so the space between marks is rest, not a miss."*

Forward-looking counters ("4 more practices open Camp 3") are allowed because they name a real,
near, deadline-free milestone. The **final** claim is never counted — it is self-claimed (§0.1).

### 4.2 The admiration router — turning what you admire into what you do

The user's own framework starts with a question Compass never asked:
*"What did I admire that I can embody rather than merely admire?"*
Without it the app helps you **practise** a quality you have to arrive already having named.

**Prompt** — must work on old material, not only this week's:

> **What have you admired — recently, or for a long time?**
> *A person, a piece of work, a way someone was in a room. It can be from this week or from twenty years ago.*

**Read-back** — Compass names at most two things, and the user chooses:

| Offered | Is | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **A quality** | what's underneath it | `Save as a seed` → seed shelf on Paths (≤ 3) |
| **A world** | the thing to go and do | *shown as text only.* Plan if it needs time set aside; otherwise just go |
| **Neither** | just an observation | `Keep the note` — nothing is added anywhere |

**Non-negotiable rules.** Safety constraints, not preferences:

1. **No names. No person field. Ever.** Nothing that could become a record of a person.
2. **Never suggests a move toward anyone.** No contact, no checking, no interpreting behaviour. It hands back *your* next move and nothing else.
3. **No history feed of admirations.** Individual notes are keepable; a scrollable log of everything you have ever admired is a rumination surface.
4. **Suggests, never adds.** Nothing enters a shelf without an explicit choice (§0 — coaching = curated choice).

### 4.2a Things to try are part of a practice, not a feature of their own
Trying something new is how you find out what you like — so it is **Curiosity's practice**,
in the same way sitting still is Stillness's. There is **no "Worlds" list, no bucket list, no
try-list screen.** An earlier draft invented one; it was a fourth home for something two
existing screens already held.

Instead, qualities whose move is *go and do a new thing* carry a **`tryPool`** — a small
categorised set of suggestions rendered inside the practice block of the detail sheet, so
the user never has to think of one cold. Currently `curiosity` (Hobbies · Body · Music ·
Reading · Places), `play`, and `beauty`.

The loop, and each step's home:

| Step | Where |
|---|---|
| Get a suggestion | **Paths** — inside the practice |
| Set time aside *(only if it needs booking)* | **Plan** — the user's choice, never automatic |
| Do it | — |
| Record what you found | **Discover** — *What you're learning you like* |

Findings are self-knowledge, so they sit beside Confirmed self-knowledge, split
**Turns out I like / Turns out I don't**. A clear *no* is a finding, not a failure.

### 4.2b How to tell whether you liked something
Liking is not self-evident, so Compass states the markers rather than asking *"did you
enjoy it?"* — which mostly measures how the last ten minutes went:

1. You thought about it afterwards without deciding to.
2. **You'd go again on your own, with nobody watching and nobody told.**
3. Time went strange — more had passed than you thought.
4. You were interested in being bad at it, not only in being good at it.

Two honest caveats, both shown:
- **Liking the thing vs liking the idea of being someone who does it.** Marker 2 is the test.
- **One go is not a verdict** — about three, especially for things you were nervous about.

> This is the only place in Compass where a rubric is given rather than left to the user,
> and it earns the exception because the alternative isn't freedom, it's a guess. It still
> makes no claim: the user records the finding.

- **Depth** — substance over surface; able to dwell with feeling, complexity and meaning. *Shows up as:* real questions, comfortable silences, unafraid of hard topics. *Practise:* one deep question/day; read slowly; resist the quick take. *Resolves:* feeling shallow or performative.

- **Groundedness** — rooted, hard to knock over. *Practise:* feet-on-floor body check; return to values under pressure. *Resolves:* being easily destabilised.

### Family — Strength & Authority

## 6. Resolved design decisions (were open questions)

1. **Earned qualities → self-claimed.** A Path/practice completing does *not* auto-earn. Compass asks *“Does this feel like you now?”* with gentle handling of **No / Not sure** (see §0.1). Self-honesty > badges.
2. **“Admired” → renamed “Drawn to”, capped at 3, anti-limerence.** Not a hoardable wishlist of lack; reframed as *seeds already in you*; only two doors (practise now / let it rest). (Principles §1.4, §2.5.)
3. **Quality ↔ Path decoupled.** A forming quality may sit behind a Path *or* just a daily cue.
4. **Exemplars — cautious yes, guarded.** OFF by default. When on, default to **archetypes/characters** (“the quiet mentor”, “the wise elder”), not real people. If a real person is ever noted, the app reframes to the mirror (*“what moves you in them is proof of it in you”*) and **never** toward proximity-seeking, comparison, or “become like them.” Exemplars point back to the self, never toward another person — this protects limerence-prone and comparison-prone users.
