How things open

Compass follows Apple Health. Hierarchy is navigated by pushing a new page with a back button. Quick looks and small decisions happen in a sheet that slides up over the page you are on. Long text is cut to three lines with Show more. Nothing else expands or collapses, so content never hides unexpectedly, and there are no accordions inside accordions.

Rules

  • At most three pushes below a tab. Paths → Growth paths → Self-awareness → quality page is the deepest route.
  • Back goes where you came from and is labelled with that page’s title (“‹ Self-awareness”, “‹ Paths”).
  • Tapping a path you are not walking opens a preview sheet, not the full page. It holds the essentials: what it is, what it gives you, the practice, where the route starts, and Start. See full page closes the sheet and pushes the quality page.
  • Tapping a path you are walking goes straight to its page. You already know it, so there is no preview.
  • Never a sheet on top of a sheet. If something needs a second step, the first sheet closes.
  • When all three slots are in use, Start becomes “All three slots are in use” with “Choose one to pause”, which opens a short action sheet listing the three. Nothing is lost by pausing.
  • Destructive actions use an alert (Delete all data). Pausing uses an action sheet, with the reassurance “Keeps every return”.
  • Motion is quiet. Pages slide, sheets rise, a new return mark fades onto the route. No confetti, no bounce, no reveal.
  • Accessible by default. Every sheet has a × as well as swipe. The route and the returns line read as text (“5 returns, Camp 2, Doing”).
