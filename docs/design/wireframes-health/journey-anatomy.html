One journey page

Growth Paths and Foundations run on the same machine: one practice, returns a few times a week, the same three camps, Steady claimed by you. The only difference is where they live (a path area, or a foundation tier). So they now share one page, drawn from one template, and a section appears only when that journey has content for it.

What a person needs the moment it opens

1 · What do I do?The practice, in one sentence, with its button right under it.
2 · When does it count?“It counts when…”, and what it does not depend on.
3 · Where am I?One route, one line of text. History is one tap away.

Everything else (what it looks like in real life, the inner sentence, daily habits, the longer explanation) is supporting material and sits below.

How Apple solves it

Health › Medications is the closest match: a thing you do on a rhythm and log. The page leads with what it is and the Log action, keeps history one tap away, and puts details and options (archive, edit) underneath. Health data pages follow the same order: one chart up top, then Highlights, then an About text cut to three lines with Show more, then a plain Options list. Nothing scrolls sideways, and nothing important is hidden behind a disclosure.
