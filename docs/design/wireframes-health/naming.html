Path areas: the whole catalogue

The four areas are named after where you practise: yourself, other people, the work, the unfamiliar. Every path in the data also has two values, which say what the quality gives you. Those are shown in grey on the right.

What the list shows

  • Mastery is the largest area: 15 of 41. It mixes the work itself (Shipping, Rare skill), decisions (Calculated risk, Saying no) and long-term building (Leverage, Stewardship). As you said, each one is a kind of mastery that gives you something concrete.
  • “Where you practise” and “what it gives you” are both true, but a name based on what you get answers the question people actually have when choosing: why would I want this?
  • Aliveness is the smallest (6) and its values cluster around joy, aliveness, adventure and becoming.
