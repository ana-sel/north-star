Foundations

The floor everything else stands on. Start anywhere.

Tier 1

Tend the Body
Support the physical floor required for everything else.
Regulate
Notice activation and settle enough to choose the next action.
Walking
Steady Your Attention
Notice when attention has been captured and deliberately return it.

Tier 2

Feel & Name
Recognise emotional experience rather than immediately acting from it.
See Clearly
Separate reality from interpretation, projection, and uncertainty.
Self-relationship
Treat yourself as worthy of care rather than using self-attack as motivation.
Know Yourself
Distinguish your values, wants, and needs from social approval.

Tier 3

Communicate
Express needs, requests, limits, and repair clearly.
Relate
Stay connected without abandoning yourself.

Tier 4

Handle Hard Things
Stay with pain, grief, uncertainty, or failure without forcing resolution.
Keep Your Word
Build reliability toward yourself and others.
Live Practically
Deal with real-world money, admin, decisions, and responsibilities.

Tier 5

Make Meaning
Connect life to values, beauty, service, purpose, and what matters.
