Execution order

What happens first — and what waits

The map contains four lanes, but no more than three receive attention at once. A lane advances only after its exit gate is met.

Default capacityAbout 7 hours/weekReduce to the minimum week when life is busy.
1
Close the open property loop

Finish and rent the Vilnius flat. Keep Compass moving in two small weekly sessions. Stocks stay automated; business experiments and the bot stay parked.

Exit gate: tenant moved in + all real costs recorded
2
Stabilise and publish

Measure three rental months, publish Compass V1 to Android, and complete the one-time portfolio allocation and automation.

Exit gate: 3 months of rental actuals + Compass publicly available
3
Test one new income engine

Run one low-contact business experiment for 2–4 weeks. Add Compass subscriptions only if repeated use appears. The trading bot remains research or paper trading.

Exit gate: 10 purchases, meaningful subscriptions, or a written “stop” decision
4
Add the next asset only after evidence

Buy property 2 only if the buy box, reserve and net-yield test pass. Scale the product that shows paid demand; do not scale two experiments together.

Exit gate: stable cash flow and one proven growth candidate
5
Scale the winner toward independence

Reinvest profits, add property one door at a time, keep the core portfolio on autopilot and protect the €1,800 net monthly freedom target.

Finish line: assets and products reliably replace employment
