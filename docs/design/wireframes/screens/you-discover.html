What you're learning you like
You find out what you like by trying things, not by thinking about them. Both answers are worth having — a clear no is as useful as a yes.
How to tell whether you actually liked it
1
You thought about it afterwards without deciding to — on the way home, or the next day.
2
You'd go again on your own, with nobody watching and nobody told.
3
Time went strange — more had passed than you thought.
4
You were interested in being bad at it, not only in being good at it.
Watch for the gap between liking the thing and liking the idea of being someone who does it. Number 2 is the test. And one go isn't a verdict — most things need about three before you know, especially the ones you were nervous about.
