# Foundations

Foundations are the floor: basic adult capacities that support life across domains. They are not Growth Paths and never diagnose, rank, or grade a person. All thirteen are first-class bundled content and may use the shared journey model: Watching, Doing, Under pressure, and self-confirmed Steady.

| Tier | Foundation | Purpose and core practice | What counts | Habit templates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tend the Body | Support the physical floor. Notice one neglected basic need and meet it in the smallest useful way. | One basic need is met. | Water, daylight, medication/supplements, movement, SPF, oral care, wind-down |
| 1 | Regulate | Notice activation, use one settling tool, then choose the next action. | One settling tool is used and a next action is chosen. | 5-minute meditation / settling |
| 1 | Steady Your Attention | Notice where attention went, choose where it goes next, return once. | Attention capture is noticed and attention returns once. | First 15 minutes phone-free |
| 2 | Feel & Name | Name a feeling and, optionally, a need. | One feeling is named; no feeling must be fixed. | None |
| 2 | See Clearly | Split a situation into Fact / Story / Unknown. | At least one fact, story, and relevant unknown are named. | None |
| 2 | Self-relationship | Answer a harsh inner statement with a fairer adult response. | The harsh statement and a fairer response are noticed. | None |
| 2 | Know Yourself | Ask what is genuinely wanted when nobody is watching. | One preference, value, or need is identified. | None |
| 3 | Communicate | Say a need, request, limit, or repair clearly. | One clear expression is made. Communicate owns basic boundary tools. | None |
| 3 | Relate | Notice what is happening for another person and what remains true for oneself. | Both perspectives have room. | None |
| 4 | Handle Hard Things | Let difficulty be true without forcing resolution. | The experience is allowed; feeling better is not required. | None |
| 4 | Keep Your Word | Keep a commitment, renegotiate early, or repair honestly. | One commitment is kept, renegotiated, or repaired. Keep Your Word owns basic follow-through. | None |
| 4 | Live Practically | Take the smallest useful practical next step. | One concrete practical action occurs. | None |
| 5 | Make Meaning | Do one small thing that expresses what matters. | One small expression of what matters occurs. | None |

Regulate may suggest slow breathing, orienting, grounding, a longer exhale, or dropping the shoulders. These are practical tools, never medical claims. Handle Hard Things is distinct from Resilience: it is staying with difficulty, while Resilience is returning to life after it.

Foundations share the three active-journey slots with Growth Paths. Daily Habits do not use a journey slot. No Foundation is hard-locked in V1.
