# Habits

Habits are small daily anchors, separate from deliberate Foundation and Growth Path returns. People add one at a time; the app never activates the library by default. Missed days never erase progress or invite shame. A completion may be unticked on the same day, and archiving preserves history.

The V1 curated template library contains exactly these nine daily Habits. Custom Habits remain allowed.

| Template | Owner | Ritual | Minimum version |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water on waking | Tend the Body | Morning | Drink some water after getting up. |
| Morning daylight | Tend the Body | Morning | Get outside or into natural daylight after waking. |
| Take my medication / supplements | Tend the Body | Morning | Take the regimen already chosen or advised. |
| Daily movement minimum | Tend the Body | Morning | Do the smallest useful amount of movement today. |
| 5-minute meditation / settling | Regulate | Morning | Sit, breathe, or settle for five minutes. |
| First 15 minutes phone-free | Steady Your Attention | Morning | Keep the phone away for the first 15 minutes after waking. |
| Morning SPF | Tend the Body | Morning | Apply chosen sun protection to face and exposed neck. |
| Evening oral care | Tend the Body | Evening | Complete the chosen evening brushing or interdental routine. |
| Wind-down before sleep | Tend the Body | Evening | Begin the chosen wind-down and reduce screen use before bed. |

Compass does not recommend medication, supplements, exercise intensity, or medical treatment. Habits are only for actions genuinely intended every day. Situational, weekly, and response-to-trigger actions belong to a Foundation, Growth Path, Plan, training schedule, or optional reminder rather than the Habit engine.
