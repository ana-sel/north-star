# Growth Paths

Growth Paths are optional journeys for one quality a person deliberately practises. The full 41-path catalogue remains bundled in the mobile app. V1 Browse exposes exactly the fourteen IDs below; all other catalogue entries are dormant.

```text
FULL_PATH_CATALOG = 41
V1_FEATURED_PATHS = 14
ACTIVE_JOURNEY_CAP = 3
```

| ID | Quality | V1 return | It counts when |
|---|---|---|---|
| `stillness` | Stillness | Pause before responding where reaction would normally be immediate. | An impulse is noticed and even a small pause is created. |
| `selfpossession` | Self-possession | Say or do something genuinely yours when tempted to perform, shrink, or impress. | One authentic action, preference, or sentence is chosen. |
| `nonattachment` | Non-attachment | Name the gripped outcome and what remains yours without it. | Both parts are named; wanting need not disappear. |
| `resilience` | Resilience | After difficulty, take one small useful next action. | The action happens while pain may remain. |
| `reciprocity` | Reciprocity | Offer once, then leave space to observe whether the other side moves. | Space is allowed rather than reciprocity manufactured. |
| `courage` | Emotional Courage | Let one feeling, preference, need, or truth be visible without a hedge. | The honest thing becomes visible. |
| `authority` | Quiet Authority | Say what you mean calmly, without a long defence. | It is stated clearly and allowed to stand. |
| `warmth` | Warmth | Express genuine warmth instead of keeping it inside. | One sincere acknowledgement, response, or kindness is expressed. |
| `seeing` | Seeing People | Ask a genuine second question and stay with the answer. | The other person's experience is not immediately turned back to oneself. |
| `grace` | Grace | Preserve dignity without abandoning a boundary. | Self-respect is kept without needless cruelty or humiliation. |
| `curiosity` | Curiosity | Follow a genuine question into what is not yet understood. | The person investigates, asks, explores, or tests. |
| `agency` | Agency | Identify one controllable move and make it. | A real action occurs. |
| `shipping` | Shipping | Put something imperfect in front of a real external feedback source. | It leaves private preparation and meets reality. |
| `play` | Play | Do something enjoyable without requiring a productive outcome. | The activity is allowed to be valuable without optimisation. |

No featured Path owns a daily Habit. Regulate owns meditation/settling and Warmth is never a required kindness quota. Boundaries belongs to the Communicate Foundation and Follow-through belongs to Keep Your Word; Presence, Craftsmanship, Tenderness, Boundaries, and Follow-through remain full-catalogue-only in V1.

The shared journey model is Watching through 3 returns, Doing through 9 total returns, and an Under pressure reflection opportunity at 15 total returns. There is at most one counted return per local day. Integration is always self-claimed after reflection opens, never awarded automatically. Growth Paths are spaced practice, not daily calendars or streaks.
