# Practice Model

Compass runs two different rhythms:

| Engine | Rhythm | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Habits and rituals | Daily, small, and stable | Practical anchors |
| Path and Foundation practices | Spaced, one deliberate theme per day | Chosen growth |

Today's load is a light ritual preview, one deliberate Path or Foundation theme, and a small number of real tasks. Rituals live in Log > Habits. The selected theme may come from a Foundation or Growth Path.

Each practice contains a clear container, an executable attentional or behavioral move, and "It counts when" language that names a controllable action rather than an outcome.

The active focus budget is shared: a maximum of three active journeys across Foundations and Paths, ideally one primary and two light supports. Daily Habits do not use a journey slot. Starting a fourth asks the person to pause an existing journey or cancel.

Every journey opens Watching at 3 returns, Doing at 9 total returns, and an Under pressure reflection at 15 total returns. The final Integrated or Steady state is always self-confirmed, never automatic.
