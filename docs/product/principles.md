# Compass Principles

Compass is a compass, not a map. It offers orientation and small practices, not a verdict on a person.

- Use adult, calm, intelligent language.
- Missed days carry no shame: "Welcome back. Nothing's lost."
- Emphasize practices and observable returns, never identity judgement.
- Keep the product local-first and privacy-respecting.
- Do not diagnose, offer therapy, or promise medical or mental-health outcomes.
- Do not punish missed streaks, add leaderboards, create fake urgency, or use addiction-style mechanics.
- Integration is always self-claimed, never automatically awarded.
- A practice counts through an action the person controls, never an external outcome.

See [engagement-model.md](engagement-model.md) for allowed engagement patterns and [practice-model.md](practice-model.md) for shared practice mechanics.
