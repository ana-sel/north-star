## Principles

Compass is a compass, not a map. It offers orientation and small practices, not a verdict on a person.

- Use adult, calm, intelligent language.
- Missed days carry no shame: "Welcome back. Nothing's lost."
- Emphasize practices and observable returns, never identity judgement.
- Keep the product local-first and privacy-respecting.
- Do not diagnose, offer therapy, or promise medical or mental-health outcomes.
- Do not punish missed streaks, add leaderboards, create fake urgency, or use addiction-style mechanics.
- Integration is always self-claimed, never automatically awarded.
- A practice counts through an action the person controls, never an external outcome.

See [engagement patterns](#engagement-model) and [shared practice mechanics](#practice-model) below.

## Practice Model

Compass runs two different rhythms:

| Engine | Rhythm | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Habits and rituals | Daily, small, and stable | Practical anchors |
| Path and Foundation practices | Spaced, one deliberate theme per day | Chosen growth |

Today's load is a light ritual preview, one deliberate Path or Foundation theme, and up to three Trello tasks when connected. Rituals live in Log > Habits. The selected theme may come from a Foundation or Growth Path.

Each practice contains a clear container, an executable attentional or behavioral move, and "It counts when" language that names a controllable action rather than an outcome.

The active focus budget is shared: a maximum of three active journeys across Foundations and Paths, ideally one primary and two light supports. Daily Habits do not use a journey slot. Starting a fourth asks the person to pause an existing journey or cancel.

Every journey opens Watching at 3 returns, Doing at 9 total returns, and an Under pressure reflection at 15 total returns. The final Integrated or Steady state is always self-confirmed, never automatic.

## Habits

Habits are small daily anchors, separate from deliberate Foundation and Growth Path returns. People add one at a time; the app never activates the library by default. Missed days never erase progress or invite shame. A completion may be unticked on the same day, and archiving preserves history.

The V1 curated template library contains exactly these nine daily Habits. Custom Habits remain allowed.

| Template | Owner | Ritual | Minimum version |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water on waking | Tend the Body | Morning | Drink some water after getting up. |
| Morning daylight | Tend the Body | Morning | Get outside or into natural daylight after waking. |
| Take my medication / supplements | Tend the Body | Morning | Take the regimen already chosen or advised. |
| Daily movement minimum | Tend the Body | Morning | Do the smallest useful amount of movement today. |
| 5-minute meditation / settling | Regulate | Morning | Sit, breathe, or settle for five minutes. |
| First 15 minutes phone-free | Steady Your Attention | Morning | Keep the phone away for the first 15 minutes after waking. |
| Morning SPF | Tend the Body | Morning | Apply chosen sun protection to face and exposed neck. |
| Evening oral care | Tend the Body | Evening | Complete the chosen evening brushing or interdental routine. |
| Wind-down before sleep | Tend the Body | Evening | Begin the chosen wind-down and reduce screen use before bed. |

Compass does not recommend medication, supplements, exercise intensity, or medical treatment. Habits are only for actions genuinely intended every day. Situational, weekly, and response-to-trigger actions belong to a Foundation, Growth Path, practical task in Trello, training schedule, or optional reminder rather than the Habit engine.

## Foundations

Foundations are the floor: basic adult capacities that support life across domains. They are not Growth Paths and never diagnose, rank, or grade a person. All thirteen are first-class bundled content and may use the shared journey model: Watching, Doing, Under pressure, and self-confirmed Steady.

| Tier | Foundation | Purpose and core practice | What counts | Habit templates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tend the Body | Support the physical floor. Notice one neglected basic need and meet it in the smallest useful way. | One basic need is met. | Water, daylight, medication/supplements, movement, SPF, oral care, wind-down |
| 1 | Regulate | Notice activation, use one settling tool, then choose the next action. | One settling tool is used and a next action is chosen. | 5-minute meditation / settling |
| 1 | Steady Your Attention | Notice where attention went, choose where it goes next, return once. | Attention capture is noticed and attention returns once. | First 15 minutes phone-free |
| 2 | Feel & Name | Name a feeling and, optionally, a need. | One feeling is named; no feeling must be fixed. | None |
| 2 | See Clearly | Split a situation into Fact / Story / Unknown. | At least one fact, story, and relevant unknown are named. | None |
| 2 | Self-relationship | Answer a harsh inner statement with a fairer adult response. | The harsh statement and a fairer response are noticed. | None |
| 2 | Know Yourself | Ask what is genuinely wanted when nobody is watching. | One preference, value, or need is identified. | None |
| 3 | Communicate | Say a need, request, limit, or repair clearly. | One clear expression is made. Communicate owns basic boundary tools. | None |
| 3 | Relate | Notice what is happening for another person and what remains true for oneself. | Both perspectives have room. | None |
| 4 | Handle Hard Things | Let difficulty be true without forcing resolution. | The experience is allowed; feeling better is not required. | None |
| 4 | Keep Your Word | Keep a commitment, renegotiate early, or repair honestly. | One commitment is kept, renegotiated, or repaired. Keep Your Word owns basic follow-through. | None |
| 4 | Live Practically | Take the smallest useful practical next step. | One concrete practical action occurs. | None |
| 5 | Make Meaning | Do one small thing that expresses what matters. | One small expression of what matters occurs. | None |

Regulate may suggest slow breathing, orienting, grounding, a longer exhale, or dropping the shoulders. These are practical tools, never medical claims. Handle Hard Things is distinct from Resilience: it is staying with difficulty, while Resilience is returning to life after it.

Foundations share the three active-journey slots with Growth Paths. Daily Habits do not use a journey slot. No Foundation is hard-locked in V1.

## Growth Paths

Growth Paths are optional journeys for one quality a person deliberately practises. The full 41-path catalogue remains bundled in the mobile app. V1 Browse exposes exactly the fourteen IDs below; all other catalogue entries are dormant.

```text
FULL_PATH_CATALOG = 41
V1_FEATURED_PATHS = 14
ACTIVE_JOURNEY_CAP = 3
```

| ID | Quality | V1 return | It counts when |
|---|---|---|---|
| `stillness` | Stillness | Pause before responding where reaction would normally be immediate. | An impulse is noticed and even a small pause is created. |
| `selfpossession` | Self-possession | Say or do something genuinely yours when tempted to perform, shrink, or impress. | One authentic action, preference, or sentence is chosen. |
| `nonattachment` | Non-attachment | Name the gripped outcome and what remains yours without it. | Both parts are named; wanting need not disappear. |
| `resilience` | Resilience | After difficulty, take one small useful next action. | The action happens while pain may remain. |
| `reciprocity` | Reciprocity | Offer once, then leave space to observe whether the other side moves. | Space is allowed rather than reciprocity manufactured. |
| `courage` | Emotional Courage | Let one feeling, preference, need, or truth be visible without a hedge. | The honest thing becomes visible. |
| `authority` | Quiet Authority | Say what you mean calmly, without a long defence. | It is stated clearly and allowed to stand. |
| `warmth` | Warmth | Express genuine warmth instead of keeping it inside. | One sincere acknowledgement, response, or kindness is expressed. |
| `seeing` | Seeing People | Ask a genuine second question and stay with the answer. | The other person's experience is not immediately turned back to oneself. |
| `grace` | Grace | Preserve dignity without abandoning a boundary. | Self-respect is kept without needless cruelty or humiliation. |
| `curiosity` | Curiosity | Follow a genuine question into what is not yet understood. | The person investigates, asks, explores, or tests. |
| `agency` | Agency | Identify one controllable move and make it. | A real action occurs. |
| `shipping` | Shipping | Put something imperfect in front of a real external feedback source. | It leaves private preparation and meets reality. |
| `play` | Play | Do something enjoyable without requiring a productive outcome. | The activity is allowed to be valuable without optimisation. |

No featured Path owns a daily Habit. Regulate owns meditation/settling and Warmth is never a required kindness quota. Boundaries belongs to the Communicate Foundation and Follow-through belongs to Keep Your Word; Presence, Craftsmanship, Tenderness, Boundaries, and Follow-through remain full-catalogue-only in V1.

The shared journey model is Watching through 3 returns, Doing through 9 total returns, and an Under pressure reflection opportunity at 15 total returns. There is at most one counted return per local day. Integration is always self-claimed after reflection opens, never awarded automatically. Growth Paths are spaced practice, not daily calendars or streaks.

## Engagement Model

> Canonical engagement guidance. Product scope is locked in [PLAN.md](../../PLAN.md).

> **Guidelines, not rules.** These are the current best answers, not a contract.
> Get the design right first, then update this file to match it.

Compass is engaging but not manipulative. The mechanisms below are the full list of allowed retention/motivation patterns.

### Core loop

```
Log today → see progress → grow qualities → identity feels real → repeat
```

### Two engines

| Engine | Rhythm | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Rituals (habits)** | Daily, automatic | Stable anchors — morning/evening. Many coexist because they're cheap. |
| **Practice (qualities)** | ~2–4×/week, one per day, rotated | Deliberate growth. Spacing beats daily grind. Never the same quality two days running. |

### Integration states (qualities & tools)

```
Drawn to  →  Practising  →  Integrated  (or Paused)
```

- **Drawn to** — hard cap of 3. Not a wishlist; reframed as "a seed already in you."
- **Practising** — active, shows on Today.
- **Integrated** — self-claimed by the user, never auto-awarded by the app.
- **Paused** — history kept, no pressure.

### 108-rep mastery

Habits track lifetime completions toward a cycle target (21 / 40 / 66 / 108 reps). Default is 108. Progress is purely informational — no punishment for slow pace.

### Qualities have no rep target

Rep cycles apply to **habits**, which are daily and countable. A **quality** has no threshold, and inventing one would be false and coercive. Lally et al. (2010) found automaticity anywhere between **18 and 254 repetitions**, averaging ~66 — the spread is the finding.

So Compass states the range, names the variance, and hands the decision back: *"There isn't a number. Compass will never tell you that you've done it. You decide that."*

- Never a bar toward 66, an "N to go" counter, or an integration percentage.
- A plain count of returns so far is fine, shown next to the honest range.
- Camp counters ("4 more practices open Camp 3") are fine — a camp is a real, near, deadline-free step. Integration itself is never counted.

### The quality changes in the inner voice

Two people assemble the same wardrobe for the same hour. One thinks *"I'm hopeless at this — who wrote these instructions?"*, the other *"Huh, interesting how that part fits. I'll find a way."* Same visible behaviour; only the second is practising anything.

Every quality therefore carries a **voice pair** — the automatic sentence and the one being practised. Rules:

- Frame the first as a **state** ("when it's tight"), never a character flaw or a wrong thought.
- Never mark it with an ✗ or red — that is deficit framing.
- The practice is *noticing which voice is speaking*, not eliminating one.

### Every practice needs "it counts when"

A practice that names only a container ("sit for 5 minutes") leaves the user to invent their own success condition — and they will pick the **outcome**, which is never theirs to control. So each practice states an achievable completion condition and explicitly rules out the outcome:

| Practice | It counts when | Explicitly *not* |
|---|---|---|
| The 5-minute sit | you came back once | when your mind goes quiet |
| Take one thing to done | you did the last tenth | when the result is good |
| Say it once, then stop | you stopped after saying it | when they agree |
| Say the kind thing | you said it out loud | when they respond well |
| Do the unfamiliar thing | you started before feeling ready | when it goes well |

This is the difference between a practice that builds someone and one that quietly grades them.

### Gradual build (anti-overwhelm)

- Onboarding starts with **one tiny habit**.
- New habits added one at a time, only when the current one is steady.
- Each habit starts at its smallest dose and escalates gradually.

### Allowed engagement patterns

- Light streaks (shown gently, never weaponised)
- Paths with camps and story progression
- Gentle visual world growth
- Soft rewards for milestones
- Meaningful check-in reflections
- Forward counters toward a real, near, deadline-free milestone ("4 more practices open Camp 3")
- **News that is true**: "You came back twice since you were last here." Only when the count is non-zero, always warm and factual — never `+2`, a badge or a score.
- **No manufactured reveal.** A shimmer-then-slot-machine "Pattern spotted" fired on every tick is a variable-reward mechanic wearing an insight's clothes, and the stat it shows was never earned by that tap. Patterns are deferred in V1; any future pattern view must carry evidence and allow disagreement. The honest reward for practising is the mark landing on your own trail.
- **Done is warmer, not dimmer.** A completed practice must not grey out, fade or strike through. Fading reads as *deactivated*, and a strikethrough says the way of being is finished with — the opposite of the point. Completion earns colour: the tick fills, the day's mark lands, and one true line names the return.

### Visible journey, honest signal

The expedition language (paths, camps, walking, terrain) has to be visible or it's just decoration on a progress bar. The path card draws a **route**: three camp nodes, a gold claim node at the end that only the user fills, and a **walker** positioned by real returns.

The rule that makes it safe: **the walker derives only from completed returns, so it can never move backwards.** A bad week costs nothing already earned. Growth accumulates; it never decays.

### Prohibited patterns

- Leaderboards or global rankings
- Shame or punishment for missed days
- Fake urgency / countdown pressure
- "Fix yourself" or deficit framing
- Addiction-style dark patterns

### Displaying spaced practice

Practice is **deliberately spaced** — 2–4× a week, never two days running — and a skip on Today is
a legitimate choice. Any display that shows the rest days as empty slots turns the design into
visible failure.

- **Never** a daily grid with empty cells, an attendance ratio ("5 of 7 days"), or a days-since counter.
- **Instead** draw only the days the user returned, on a plain line, and say why the gaps are there.
- After a week away the tone stops counting: **"Resting — nothing's lost."**

Rituals (daily habits) are a different rhythm and may be tracked densely — they live in Log · Habits.
A path may carry no ritual at all, and one ritual may be shared by several paths.

### Missed-day tone

> "Welcome back. Nothing's lost."
> Never: "You broke your streak."

## Design System

The warm neutral background is intentional. White cards provide contrast, while accents communicate semantic meaning. Path domain colours must remain visible: the deployed APK must not collapse into a beige-only interface.

Follow the HTML wireframes' hierarchy, spacing, and density closely. Android may adapt only where native interaction or accessibility requires it.

Source of truth: `docs/design/wireframes/shared/`
